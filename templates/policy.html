Frequently Asked Questions

Are ISVANTE watches water resistant?

Yes. Our dress watches are rated at 5 ATM (50 meters splash/rain resistant), and our Diver series is rated up to 20 ATM (200 meters) with screw-down crowns.

Can I inspect the parcel before paying on COD?

Yes. Couriers permit external box verification to ensure the tamper-evident seal is intact before exchanging payment.

What movements are used inside ISVANTE timepieces?

We use authentic Japanese Seiko (NH35A automatic, VK63 mecha-quartz) and Miyota (Citizen Watch Co.) calibers renowned for bulletproof reliability and accurate timekeeping.

Does ISVANTE provide an official warranty in Pakistan?

Every watch carries an official 1-Year ISVANTE Nationwide Warranty stamped on an embossed authenticity card.
